# Catalog image provenance

The public asset directory contains 30 generated photographs of a consistent fictional slim male model, on a neutral grey seamless studio backdrop, wearing the 30 complete outfits in data.js. No brand logos or text. Each is an individual 800×1200 WebP.

The owner’s separate 30-look personal catalog was generated from his supplied photographs and latest accepted face/body references. Those images and their reference assets live only in the private server checkout, never in this public static bundle. The server requires verified Firebase authorization before returning personal pixels.

## Public welcome photography

`dist/welcome/` contains three AI-generated editorial lifestyle images of fictional people, created without personal reference photos. They are separate from the 30-look catalog and labelled as fictional AI imagery on the homepage.

- `generations.png`: an older silver-haired woman and a younger East Asian woman, in sage, cream and navy casual clothing.
- `families.png`: a Black father holding a fully clothed baby beside his young daughter, in comfortable age-appropriate clothing.
- `together.png`: two adult men, a gay couple holding hands, with distinct everyday styles and natural body proportions.

Shared direction: premium minimalist full-body studio photography, pale warm grey seamless backdrop, soft diffused light, restrained palette, real textile texture, relaxed expressions, no logos or text. The site states that the current interactive collection is a menswear sample and that women's and children's collections are not yet available.
